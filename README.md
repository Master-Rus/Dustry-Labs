## What is Dustry Labs?

Build networks of interconnected modules, automate processes, unlock upgrades, and experiment with new ways to grow your system — all directly in your browser.

- **Build freely** — connect modules and create your own setups.
- **Automate** — optimize complex production chains.
- **Keep progressing** — discover upgrades, features, and bonuses.
- **Save your progress** — sign in to continue from your cloud save.
